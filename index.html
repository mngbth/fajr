Fajr Quran Class

A simple Quran class for children aged 2–12.

We focus on learning and recognizing Arabic letters.

Children learn through reading, writing, coloring, playtime, and small rewards.

We also introduce Quran reading, Thajweedh, and memorization step by step.

Our goal is simple: every child can recognize and read letters with confidence.
